Mini Sudoku Solver – Today's 6×6 Sudoku Answer, Step by Step

Stuck on a mini sudoku? Type in the starting numbers, press Solve and reveal the answer one square at a time – each with the reason it is forced. Works for 6×6 and classic 9×9 boards.

Stuck on today's Mini Sudoku? Get the answer in three steps

  1. Keep 6×6 selected. The daily Mini Sudoku on LinkedIn is a 6×6 board with six 2×3 boxes. Switch to 9×9 for a classic sudoku from a newspaper or app.
  2. Copy the starting numbers. Tap a square and type its number. Empty squares stay empty – just tap past them. A number entered twice in a row, column or box is shown in red straight away.
  3. Press Solve. The first forced square turns green with a short explanation. Press Show next square for another nudge, or Show full answer to see everything.

How the Mini Sudoku solver works

The solver uses the same two moves a person uses, in the same order. First it looks for hidden singles: inside one row, column or box, a number that fits in only one empty square. Then it looks for naked singles: a square whose row, column and box already contain every number except one. Each time it places a number, it remembers which move proved it, and it repeats until the board is full.

Almost every 6×6 puzzle is finished this way. For a hard 9×9 board that needs more advanced logic, the solver switches to a fast search that tries the square with the fewest candidates first. Either way, it also checks for a second solution and tells you if there is one – the usual sign that a starting number was missed.

Because each step names its row, column or box, the list of steps doubles as a lesson: look at the place it mentions and try to see why the number is forced before revealing the next one.

Worked example

Press Load example to put one of our own Hard 6×6 boards on the solver – it starts with only nine numbers – then press Solve. The first step reads "Row 1, column 4: 2 – the only square in row 1 where 2 fits". Look along row 1: every other empty square in it already sees a 2, either in its own column or inside its 2×3 box, so the 2 has nowhere else to go. The next three steps all happen in row 5 in the same way, and each new number opens up the column and box around it. Scanning one number along one row, column or box is enough to finish this whole board. On other boards you will also meet steps that say "its row, column and box already hold every other number": count the numbers around that square and only one is missing.

Solve it yourself next time

Two habits crack most mini sudokus: sweep each number from 1 to 6 across the six boxes, and check any row, column or box that has only one or two empty squares left. Practise them on fresh boards in Mini Sudoku Unlimited – every board there has exactly one answer, and the hint explains each move.

FAQ

How do I get the answer to today's Mini Sudoku?

Keep 6×6 selected, tap the first square that has a number in your puzzle and type it (or use the number pad). The cursor moves on automatically. When every starting number is in, press Solve: the solver shows one square at a time with the reason, or the full answer at once.

Why does the solver say there is no solution?

One of the numbers was entered in the wrong square, or a number is missing. Compare the board row by row with your puzzle – a single misplaced number is enough to make it impossible.

Why does it say there is more than one answer?

A proper sudoku has exactly one solution. If the solver finds two, a starting number was left out. Add the missing number and solve again.

Can I just get a hint instead of the whole answer?

Yes. After Solve, only the first square is filled, together with the reason it is forced. Press Show next square for one more at a time.

Does it solve normal 9×9 sudoku too?

Yes. Choose 9×9 in the size box. The solver uses the same logical steps first and switches to a fast search if a very hard 9×9 puzzle needs more advanced techniques.

Is my board sent anywhere?

No. The solver runs completely in your browser. Nothing you type is uploaded or stored on our servers.
