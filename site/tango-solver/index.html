How the Tango solver works

The solver thinks in whole lines, the way experienced players do. For every row and column it lists all the arrangements that are still allowed: the right number of suns and moons, never three in a row, matching every symbol already on the board and every sign inside that line. If one square has the same symbol in all of those arrangements, that symbol is forced – the solver writes it in and remembers which line proved it.

Every new symbol also narrows the crossing column or row, so the solver keeps going round the board until nothing changes. Proper Tango puzzles are finished this way without a single guess. If a board cannot be completed by logic alone, the solver switches to a careful search, and it tells you if it finds no answer or more than one – the most common sign that something was entered wrongly.

Because each step names its row or column, the list of steps doubles as a lesson: look at the line it mentions and try to see why the square is forced before you reveal the next one.

Solve it yourself next time

Most boards fall to three habits: put the opposite symbol at both ends of every pair, fill the gap between two equal symbols, and finish any line that already has its full share of one symbol. FAQ

How do I get the answer to today's Tango puzzle?

Choose the board size (the daily LinkedIn board is 6×6), tap each square that already shows a sun or moon until it matches, and tap the small circles between squares to add the = and × signs. Press Solve: the solver fills one square at a time and tells you which row or column forces it, or shows the full answer at once.

How do I enter the = and × signs?

Between every two neighbouring squares there is a small circle. Tap it once for =, twice for ×, and a third time to remove the sign again.

Why does the solver say there is no solution?

One of the symbols or signs was entered differently from your puzzle. Compare row by row – a single wrong sign is enough to make the board impossible.

Why does it say there is more than one answer?

Real Tango boards have exactly one solution. If the solver finds two, part of the puzzle is missing – usually a sign or a starting symbol that was not entered.

Can I just get a hint instead of the whole answer?

Yes. After Solve, only the first square is shown, together with the reason. Press Show next square for more, one at a time.

What sizes does the Tango solver support?

4×4, 6×6, 8×8 and 10×10 boards – every even size used by Tango, Takuzu and binary puzzles with balanced rows and columns.

Is my board sent anywhere?

No. The solver runs completely in your browser. Nothing you enter is uploaded or stored on our servers.
