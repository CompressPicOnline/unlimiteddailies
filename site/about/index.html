About Unlimited Dailies

Unlimited Dailies is a small independent website that makes free, unlimited practice versions of popular daily puzzle styles. Our games are Sumbreak Unlimited, a number-merge logic puzzle with a solver and a strategy guide, Queens Unlimited, a colour-region queen-placement puzzle with its own Queens solver, Tango Unlimited, a sun-and-moon binary grid with a Tango solver, and Zip Unlimited, a numbered path puzzle with a Zip solver.

All puzzles are created by our own generator, and every board is checked to be solvable. The site is free to use and is supported by advertising.

We are not affiliated with LinkedIn, Microsoft, The New York Times or any other game publisher. Game names are used only to describe the style of puzzle.
