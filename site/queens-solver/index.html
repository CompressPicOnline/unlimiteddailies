Queens Solver – Today's Queens Answer, One Queen at a Time

Stuck on a Queens puzzle? Paint the colour regions below, press Solve, and reveal the queens one by one – or all at once.

Pick a colour, then tap or drag over the squares of one region. Repeat for every region. Colours do not have to match the game – only the shapes matter.

Stuck on today's Queens? Get the answer in three steps

  1. Pick the size. Count the rows of your puzzle – daily boards are usually between 7×7 and 10×10 – and choose that size.
  2. Paint the regions. Choose colour 1 and paint every square of one region, then colour 2 for the next region, and so on. Dragging your finger or mouse paints many squares at once; the eraser fixes slips.
  3. Press Solve. The first queen appears on the board. Use Show next queen if you only want a nudge, or Show all queens for the full answer.

How the Queens solver works

The solver places queens row by row. In each row it only tries columns that are still free, in regions that do not have a queen yet, and never next to the queen in the row above. After every placement it checks that each region without a queen still has at least one usable square further down the board; if one does not, that whole branch is dropped immediately. This pruning means even an 11×11 board is solved in a fraction of a second on a phone.

The solver also keeps looking for a second solution. Real Queens puzzles have exactly one answer, so if your board has more than one, the solver tells you – a strong sign that a square was painted with the wrong colour.

When it shows the answer, it starts with the queen in the smallest region. That is the same place a good human solver looks first, so revealing a single queen is often enough to get you going again.

Solve it yourself next time

Three habits solve most boards without help: start with the smallest region, cross out the row and column of any region that sits completely in one line, and after every queen mark all eight squares around it. Practise them on fresh boards in Queens Unlimited – every board there is checked to have exactly one solution.

FAQ

How do I get the answer to today's Queens puzzle?

Choose the board size that matches your puzzle, pick a colour and paint each region of the grid the same way it looks in the game (drag across squares to paint quickly). Press Solve and the solver reveals one queen at a time, or all of them at once.

Do my colours have to match the official colours?

No. Only the shape of each region matters. Use any colour for any region – just make sure squares that belong to the same region get the same colour and that you use exactly as many colours as there are rows.

Why does the solver say there is no solution?

Almost always one square was painted with the wrong colour. Compare the board row by row with your puzzle, fix the square and press Solve again.

What sizes does the Queens solver support?

Every square board from 5×5 up to 11×11, which covers all common Queens and star battle puzzles with one queen per row.

Can I get a hint instead of the full answer?

Yes. After you press Solve, only one queen is shown – the one in the smallest region, where you would usually start. Press Show next queen to reveal more, one at a time.

Is my board sent anywhere?

No. The solver runs completely in your browser. Nothing you paint is uploaded or saved on our servers.
