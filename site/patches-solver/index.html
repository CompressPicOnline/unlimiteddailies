How the Patches solver works

For every clue the solver first lists every rectangle that could belong to it: rectangles that contain the clue, contain no other clue, stay inside the board and match the clue's area and shape. A corner clue with a small number might have only one or two such rectangles; an "any shape" clue in the middle of the board can have dozens.

It then fills the board the way a careful player would. It always works on the clue with the fewest rectangles still available, places one, and checks that every empty square can still be reached by at least one remaining clue. If a square becomes unreachable, that choice was wrong and the solver steps back. Because it tries the most constrained clue first, real puzzles are solved in a fraction of a second.

After the first answer is found, the solver keeps looking for a second one. Proper Patches boards have exactly one solution, so if it finds two it tells you – the most common sign that a shape or number was entered differently from your puzzle.

The revealed order is chosen to teach: each step names the clue and how many rectangles it still had at that moment. "The only patch that still fits" is a deduction you can make yourself next time.

Solve it yourself next time

Most boards fall to three habits: start with corner and edge clues, place clues whose shape and number allow just one outline, and look for empty squares that only one clue can reach. Practise them on fresh boards in Patches Unlimited – every board there has exactly one solution, and the hint tells you which clue is the most constrained.

FAQ

How do I get the answer to today's Patches puzzle?

Choose the board size, tap each square that holds a clue in your puzzle, type its number (leave it empty if the clue has no number) and pick its shape. Press Solve: the answer appears one patch at a time, starting with the clue that has the fewest options, or all at once with Show full answer.

Which shape should I pick for a clue?

Square for a clue whose patch has equal sides, Wide for wider than tall, Tall for taller than wide, and Any when the clue allows every rectangle. If you are unsure, Any always works – the solver just has more to check.

Why does the solver say there is no solution?

A clue was probably entered with the wrong number or shape, or a clue is missing. If all clues have numbers, they must add up to the number of squares on the board – the solver checks that first.

Why does it say there is more than one answer?

Real Patches boards have exactly one solution. If the solver finds two, part of the puzzle is missing – usually a shape entered as Any that is really wide, tall or square.

What sizes does the Patches solver support?

Boards from 4×4 to 10×10, with any mix of numbered and numberless clues.

Is my board sent anywhere?

No. The solver runs completely in your browser, in a background thread so the page stays responsive. Nothing you enter is uploaded or stored.
