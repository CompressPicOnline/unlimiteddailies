The Sumbreak game in a nutshell

Sumbreak is a number-merge logic puzzle: drag a number onto an identical neighbour and the two tiles become their sum, leaving an empty square behind. Keep merging until only one tile is left. Tiles never slide, so every merge is a decision – merge in the wrong direction and a number can end up with no partner at all. Newer boards add wildcard tiles (×2 and +N) that can merge with any number.

The daily puzzle above is the same for everyone today and changes at midnight UTC. For more, play unlimited Sumbreak boards from an easy 3×3 to a 6×6 expert grid, learn the patterns in our rules and strategy guide, or check a tricky position with the step-by-step solver.

Daily puzzle FAQ

When does the daily puzzle change?

A new daily puzzle appears every day at midnight UTC. Everyone who visits on the same day gets the same 4×4 board with one wildcard tile, so you can compare times with friends.

Is this the same puzzle as the official LinkedIn Sumbreak?

No. Our daily board is created by our own generator, so it is a different puzzle from the one on LinkedIn. It is a free extra puzzle to warm up with or to play after you have finished the official one.

Can I play yesterday's daily puzzle?

Yes. Use the daily puzzle archive above: every past daily is listed by number and date, and you can replay any of them as often as you like.

How does the daily streak work?

Solve today's puzzle to add one day to your streak. Miss a day and the streak starts again from one. Your streak is stored only in this browser, so it does not follow you to another device.
