What is Unlimited Dailies?

Daily puzzle games are a great five-minute habit, but one puzzle a day is rarely enough – especially when you are learning a new game. Unlimited Dailies builds free practice versions of popular daily puzzle styles, with our own puzzle generator, so you can play as many rounds as you want and get better before tomorrow's official puzzle.

  • Unlimited rounds at different difficulty levels.
  • Always solvable puzzles, with hints and undo.
  • Fast and private – everything runs in your browser.

We are starting with Sumbreak, the newest daily number puzzle, and will add more puzzle styles soon.
